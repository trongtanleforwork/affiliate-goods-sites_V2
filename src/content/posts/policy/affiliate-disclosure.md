At **OkeyReview Goods** ([https://goods.okeyreview.com/](https://goods.okeyreview.com/)), our goal is to help consumers make confident purchasing decisions by publishing authentic, research-backed, and hands-on reviews of physical consumer products.

In full transparency with our readers and in strict compliance with the **Federal Trade Commission (FTC) 16 CFR Part 255 ("Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising")**, this document details our affiliate relationships, monetization policies, and editorial independence.

---

## 1. FTC Affiliate Disclosure Statement

Links on **OkeyReview Goods** are affiliate links. This means that if you click on a product link or button and make a qualifying purchase on an external merchant website, we may earn an affiliate commission at **no additional cost to you**.

```
[ Reader Clicks Product Link ] ──► [ Merchant Platform (e.g. Amazon) ] ──► [ Reader Completes Order ]
                                                                                   │
                                                                                   ▼
                                                   [ Merchant Pays OkeyReview a Referral Fee at $0 Extra Cost to Reader ]
```

---

## 2. Amazon Associates Program Disclosure

---

## 4. How Commissions Affect You (The Consumer)

* **Zero Price Inflation:** Clicking an affiliate link does **NOT** increase the price of the item. You pay the exact same retail price—or lower if we provide an exclusive promotional discount code.
* **Pricing & Availability:** Prices, discount codes, stock availability, and shipping terms displayed on merchant websites fluctuate dynamically and are outside our direct control. Always verify the final checkout total on the merchant's checkout page.

---

## 5. Editorial Independence & Rating Integrity

Our commercial relationships have **zero influence** on our editorial content:

* **Independent Testing:** Products are evaluated based on physical build quality, material safety, practical performance, and consumer value.
* **Balanced Verdicts:** We highlight real drawbacks and limitations in every review. We never publish positive ratings solely because a high commission rate is offered.
* **No Sponsored Bias:** If we test a substandard product, we will not recommend it, regardless of commercial affiliate opportunities.
