## 2. Information We Collect

## 3. Cookies, Tracking & Affiliate Attribution

OkeyReview Goods participates in affiliate programs (including Amazon Associates, ShareASale, CJ Affiliate, and Impact). 

### How Affiliate Tracking Works:
1. When you click an outbound link to a merchant site (e.g., Amazon.com), a tracking identifier or cookie is placed on your browser by the merchant network.
2. This cookie allows the merchant to verify that you originated from OkeyReview Goods if you complete a qualifying purchase within a designated tracking window (typically 24 hours to 90 days).
3. **These affiliate cookies contain no personally identifiable financial information (no credit card numbers, passwords, or personal billing addresses).**

### Managing Cookie Preferences:
You have complete control over cookies. You can configure your browser to block third-party cookies, alert you when cookies are set, or delete existing cookies via your browser's security settings.

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Compliance)

For visitors located in the European Economic Area (EEA) and United Kingdom, we process data based on:
* **Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR):** To ensure website security, prevent fraud, optimize Core Web Vitals speed, and analyze overall readership trends.
* **Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR):** When you voluntarily contact us or consent to optional tracking cookies.

## 5. Your Rights Under GDPR (EEA/UK Residents)

If you are an EU/EEA or UK resident, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:
* **Right of Access:** Request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
* **Right to Rectification:** Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
* **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** Request deletion of your personal contact data.
* **Right to Restrict or Object to Processing:** Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
* **Right to Data Portability:** Request transfer of your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise these rights, email us at [info.okeyreview@gmail.com](mailto:info.okeyreview@gmail.com).

## 6. Your Rights Under CCPA / CPRA (California Residents)

California residents possess specific consumer privacy rights:
* **Right to Know:** You may request details on categories and specific pieces of personal information collected.
* **Right to Delete:** You may request deletion of personal information collected from you.
* **Right to Opt-Out of Sale / Sharing:** **We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration.** However, we respect global privacy controls (GPC signals) and opt-out requests for third-party cookie sharing.
* **Right to Non-Discrimination:** We will not discriminate against you in pricing or service quality for exercising your CCPA rights.
