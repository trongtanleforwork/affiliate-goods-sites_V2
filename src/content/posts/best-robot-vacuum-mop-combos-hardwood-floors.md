## Comprehensive Buyer's Guide: What to Look For

1. **Rotary Spinning Pads vs. Vibrating Sonic Plates vs. Passive Pads:** For hardwood floors with textured grain, **dual rotary spinning pads** provide significantly better stain lifting than flat passive pads, which merely wipe dust rather than scrub.
2. **Auto Mop-Lifting Height:** If your home features a combination of hardwood and area rugs, look for at least **8mm to 10mm of automatic mop lifting** so the vacuum can transition seamlessly without wetting rugs.
3. **Warm Air Mop Drying:** Never buy an automated mopping robot without a heated warm-air drying dock. Air-drying wet microfiber pads in an enclosed dark dock creates foul-smelling mildew within 48 hours.
4. **Electronic Water Flow Regulation:** Ensure the companion smartphone application allows precise, customizable water output settings per room (e.g., Ultra-Low for natural wood, Medium for glazed tile).

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: Is it safe to use specialized floor cleaning solutions in robot water tanks?
Only use manufacturer-approved, low-foaming cleaning solutions (such as Roborock or Dreame official formulas). Standard acidic or sudsy floor cleaners (like bleach or pine oils) can corrode internal water valves, clog pump lines, and void the warranty.

### Q2: How often do I need to refill the clean water and empty the dirty water tank?
In a standard 1,500 to 2,000 sq. ft. home running 3 mopping sessions per week, you will typically refill the clean water tank and empty the dirty water tank once every 5 to 7 days.

### Q3: Will dark wood or black area rugs confuse the robot's cliff sensors?
Modern flagship robots like the Roborock Q Revo MaxV utilize enhanced optical and infrared cliff sensors designed to differentiate between true drop-offs (like stairwells) and dark wood stains or black carpet patterns, avoiding the "cliff sensor error" traps of older models.
