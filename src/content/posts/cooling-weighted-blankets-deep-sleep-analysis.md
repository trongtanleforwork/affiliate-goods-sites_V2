Weighted blankets have gained widespread recognition in sleep science and occupational therapy for their ability to promote deep restorative sleep, reduce sensory restlessness, and alleviate bedtime anxiety through **Deep Pressure Stimulation (DPS)**. However, the biggest complaint from consumers—especially warm sleepers and menopausal individuals—is night sweats. Traditional weighted blankets act as thermal traps: bulky polyester batting and thick plush covers insulate body heat, causing sleepers to wake up drenched in sweat.

## In-Depth Analysis & Hands-On Testing

### 1. Deep Pressure Stimulation (DPS) & Weight Distribution
Deep Pressure Stimulation works by applying gentle, evenly distributed physical pressure across the body, stimulating the autonomic nervous system to down-regulate sympathetic "fight-or-flight" cortisol levels while increasing parasympathetic production of serotonin and melatonin.

The effectiveness of this pressure depends on **pocket baffle engineering**:
* In budget weighted blankets with wide 6x6-inch pockets, beads slide to the corners, creating heavy bunches that roll off the body.
* The **Luna** and **YnM** blankets utilize dense 4x4-inch micro-quilting with double-stitched channels that keep medical-grade micro-glass beads distributed uniformly across shoulders, hips, and legs throughout the night.

## Comprehensive Buyer's Guide: What to Look For

1. **The 10% Body Weight Rule:** As a gold standard, select a blanket that weighs approximately **10% of your total body weight plus 1 to 2 pounds** (e.g., a 150 lb individual should choose a 15 lb blanket; a 200 lb individual should choose a 20 lb blanket).
2. **Micro-Glass Beads vs. Plastic Poly Pellets:** Always choose **fine micro-glass beads**. Glass beads are significantly denser and smaller than plastic pellets, meaning the blanket remains thin, noiseless when shifting positions, and free from toxic off-gassing.
3. **Bamboo Viscose / TENCEL vs. Minky Polyester:** Avoid blankets made from "minky" or "sherpa" fleece if you sleep warm. Look strictly for **Bamboo Viscose**, **Eucalyptus TENCEL**, or **100% Percale Cotton**.
4. **Single-Body Sizing:** Weighted blankets should cover your body, not the entire perimeter of the bed mattress. If a weighted blanket hangs over the bed edges, gravity will pull the blanket onto the floor overnight.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: Can two people share a weighted blanket in a Queen or King bed?
It is generally recommended that each partner uses their own individual single/twin-size weighted blanket. Sharing a weighted blanket often causes the heavier individual to pull the weight toward their side, resulting in uneven pressure distribution and sleep disruption.

### Q2: Will a weighted blanket make my feet and ankles hurt?
If you sleep on your back, heavy blankets can sometimes press feet downward (plantar flexion). If you experience foot pressure, pull the bottom of the blanket up to your ankles, or choose a lighter 10-to-12 lb blanket.

### Q3: Are cooling weighted blankets safe for children?
Weighted blankets should only be used by children aged 4 and older who can independently remove the blanket from their face without assistance. Never use weighted bedding in infant cribs or for toddlers under 3 years of age.
