## Our Mission & Editorial Independence

With hundreds of thousands of products promoted online, discerning genuine quality from misleading marketing hype has become increasingly difficult. 

Our mission at OkeyReview Goods is simple: **to provide transparent, research-backed, and real-world tested buying recommendations.**

### Editorial Principles:
* **Zero Paid Positive Ratings:** We do not accept payment to give a product a positive rating or alter our testing verdicts.
* **Unbiased Scoring:** If a product exhibits poor durability, misleading manufacturer claims, or substandard materials, we state it clearly.
* **Consumer-First Perspective:** We prioritize user safety, long-term build reliability, and fair price-to-performance value.

## How We Test & Evaluate Physical Products (E-E-A-T Standards)

Every review and buying guide on OkeyReview Goods follows our structured **5-Pillar Evaluation Protocol**:

```
[ Research & Ingredient/Material Analysis ]
                   ↓
[ Hands-On Unboxing & Build Inspection ]
                   ↓
[ Practical Real-World Performance Testing ]
                   ↓
[ Long-Term Durability & Safety Check ]
                   ↓
[ Price-to-Value Ratio & Final Verdict ]
```

### 1. Build Quality & Material Inspection
We analyze the physical materials used—including housing plastics, lens optics, metal alloys, stitching, and finishing. For electrical and battery-powered goods, we inspect port durability, thermal dissipation, and safety certifications (CE, FCC, RoHS).

### 2. Real-World Practical Utility
Products are tested in realistic living and working environments. For example, star projectors are evaluated across varied ceiling heights and room ambient lighting conditions; beauty tools are assessed for ergonomic grip and ease of sanitation; automotive accessories are evaluated for ease of installation and stability under vehicle vibrations.

### 3. Formulation & Safety Verification
For personal care and beauty items, we review ingredient lists, preservative systems, and dermatological guidelines. We strongly emphasize patch-testing protocols and identify potential allergens or irritants.

### 4. Objective Pros & Cons
We highlight the clear advantages and honest limitations of every product. No product is flawless, and knowing potential drawbacks before purchasing is essential for consumer satisfaction.

### 5. Value & Competitive Benchmarking
We compare each item against category competitors at similar price points to determine whether the product justifies its retail price tag.
